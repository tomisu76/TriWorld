# TriWorld project research evidence

This directory stores project-specific, dated, sanitized research outputs and
evidence. It is not the reusable instruction library.

Use [`../resources/README.md`](../resources/README.md) for research methods,
primary-source lists, search queries, tool guidance, checklists, and blank
templates.

Expected maintained outputs include:

- `environment-report.md`
- `evidence-ledger.md`
- `version-lock.md`
- `source-manifest.csv`
- dated decisions, canary findings, and NotebookLM exports where appropriate

Do not put downloaded game content, secrets, caches, personal data, or entire
third-party datasets here. Record a source URL, version, license, checksum, and
reproduction recipe instead.
